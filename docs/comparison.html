Honest comparison

Pick the right tool for the job.

ColdTrail is not a replacement for every OSINT tool. It is a local database, correlation engine, evidence store, and three-interface workbench around username and email pivots. Here is how it differs from the tools people usually ask about.

When to use which

ColdTrail

You need a local, repeatable investigation: pivot, harvest, correlate, store evidence, diff runs, and export a dossier.

Sherlock / Maigret

You only need a fast username sweep and do not need a database, correlation, or evidence trail.

theHarvester

You are mapping a domain's emails, hosts, and public infrastructure rather than a person.

SpiderFoot

You want a broad module-driven scan of a domain or IP and are comfortable with its web UI.

Maltego

You need a commercial graph workspace and are prepared to manage transforms and licensing.

Combination

Use ColdTrail as the local system of record and import findings from the others with coldtrail ingest or coldtrail run.
